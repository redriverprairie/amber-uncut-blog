My ideal client is a professional man who likes to stay sharp: clean-cut, clean-shaven, business-ready. That's my lane, that's my craft, and that's where I do my best work. I love those classic, professional styles that make you look put-together, confident, and ready for whatever the day throws at you.

But looks aren't the only thing. I like a client who can sit in my chair and talk a little shit, laugh, cut up, and make the appointment feel like a break instead of a chore. The ones who are successful, driven, and still know how to have a good time: that's the sweet spot. That's when the haircut hits different.

Now listen, if you're not that guy, you're still welcome. I'll still take care of you. You might not crack a joke, you might only give me a little chuckle, and you might leave missing a piece of hair. I told you I was gonna talk a little shit. Just kidding. Mostly.

But seriously, I love what I do. I love making men feel good, feel welcome, and feel ready for the world. Whether you're the ideal client or not, you're always invited to sit in my chair and walk out looking your best.
